Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about managing your poultry farm with PFMS AI.

How does the AI prediction model work for egg yields?

Our AI algorithm analyzes historical flock data, daily feed consumption, water intake, temperature, and breed-specific laying curves to forecast egg production 7–14 days in advance, helping you identify low-performance batches early.

Can I integrate this with my current CCTV cameras?

Camera availability depends on your farm setup and plan. Contact the PFMS team to discuss compatibility and supported integrations.

What kind of support do you offer?

Support options vary by plan, from community support for starter farms to priority consultation for enterprise customers.
